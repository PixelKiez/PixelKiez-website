3. Contact form and email

When you get in touch through the contact form, the following mandatory details are processed:

  • name
  • one contact detail: email address or telephone number – either one is sufficient

For an enquiry through the website analysis form, the following details are mandatory:

  • address of the website to be reviewed
  • name
  • email address to which we send the result

The following details are optional:

  • company
  • starting point (a selection describing the state of your website; contact form only)
  • your request or comments (free text)

Technically, the page from which the enquiry was sent is also transmitted and, if you previously selected a service package, which one. These details serve solely to attribute the enquiry; no usage profile is created from them.

If you enter the address of a website on the homepage, it is passed to the website analysis page in the address bar and pre-filled there. It may therefore appear in the technical log data (section 2).

The form contains a field for spam protection which is not visible to visitors and is not meant to be filled in. If it is filled in nonetheless, the enquiry is discarded and not processed further.

If you contact us directly by email, the email address, the content of the message and any further details you volunteer are processed.

The data is used to handle the enquiry, to answer follow-up questions and, where applicable, to prepare a quotation.

Where the enquiry relates to entering into or performing a contract, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For general business enquiries, processing takes place on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in handling and answering the enquiry.

The mandatory details are required so that we can handle the enquiry; without them, an enquiry through the respective form is not possible. In the contact form, email address and telephone number are equally valid – which of the two you provide is your choice. For the website analysis we need an email address, because we send the result by email. The remaining details are optional and may be left blank.

4. Cookies, consent and Google services

When you first visit our website, we ask for your consent in a notice. The two Google services named below are only loaded once you have consented, via “Accept all” or individually under “Customise settings”; before that, no connection to Google is established. If you choose “Essential only” or “Decline”, nothing is loaded from Google. We do not use any marketing or advertising services. Fonts are served exclusively from our own server; there is no connection to Google Fonts.

The provider of both services is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Data may also be transferred to Google LLC in the USA. The transfer is based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google is certified.

Google Analytics (statistics). With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to evaluate how our website is used, for example which pages are visited, for how long and on which type of device. Google Analytics sets cookies for this (_ga and a cookie whose name begins with _ga_; storage period up to 2 years) and transmits your IP address and usage data to Google. Google signals and advertising features are deactivated. Google processes the analytics data on our behalf on the basis of Google’s data processing terms (Art. 28 GDPR). The usage data stored in Google Analytics is deleted automatically no later than 2 months after your last visit. If you withdraw your consent, the analytics cookies in your browser are deleted.

Google “Preferred source” button (external media). On the pages of our knowledge section (/en/knowledge/) we offer a button with which you can save Pixelkiez as a preferred source in Google Search. After your consent, your IP address and technical information about your browser are transmitted to Google. If you are signed in to Google, Google can link the visit to your account; Google may set or read its own cookies in the process. Without consent, the button is not shown.

The legal basis in each case is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG).

Further information:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Storing your decision. Whether you accepted or declined is stored with a date in your browser’s local storage (localStorage), so that the notice does not reappear on every visit. This information does not leave your device. Storing it is technically necessary (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

Withdrawal. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future via the “Cookie settings” entry in the footer of our pages. The lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal remains unaffected.
